# Sprint 1 Retrospective

## Summary

Sprint 1 focused on establishing the foundation of Wits Quest: authentication, project scaffolding, and initial schema. By the end of the sprint we had working sign-up and sign-in flows, but very little gameplay functionality.

**Outcome:** 63% from the client review.

## What went well

- Authentication was implemented end-to-end (email/password, OAuth providers, session handling).
- Initial project structure was set up (Next.js frontend, Express backend, Supabase).
- The team established a shared understanding of the tech stack and worked through the first integration issues together.

## What didn't go well

- **Scope was miscalculated.** We thought we could deliver more than we realistically could in the time available.
- **Communication gaps within the group.** Progress was inconsistent across members, and some work was duplicated or stalled.
- **Insufficient meetings.** We didn't meet frequently enough to catch blockers early.
- **Documentation was thin.** The design and schema docs were incomplete, which made it hard for reviewers and teammates to understand the system.
- **Client expectation mismatch.** The client expected the basic user stories to be finished; we delivered authentication only.

## Actions for Sprint 2

- **Increase meeting frequency.** Hold a mid-sprint check-in to catch blockers early.
- **Improve group communication.** Use a shared channel for daily updates and blockers.
- **Prioritise the basic user stories** so that the client sees tangible gameplay progress.
- **Invest in documentation.** Make the design and schema docs thorough enough to stand on their own without a verbal walkthrough.
- **Raise the quality bar.** Aim for a significant improvement over the 63% client score.
